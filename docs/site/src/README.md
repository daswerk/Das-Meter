# Das-Meter

Das-Meter shows live Meters for what your computer plays, or for single
tracks from your DAW: **Waveform**, **Spectrum**, **Spectrogram**, **Loudness
Meter**, **Stereometer**, **Phase Scope**, **Cepstrum**, **Oscilloscope** and
**Tonal Balance**, **Tuner** and **Stereo Bands**.
It runs on its own, next to any app or DAW, on macOS and Linux, with Windows
to come. It's free and open source (MIT OR Apache-2.0).

![Das-Meter in the Smooth Look](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/daswerk/Das-Meter/main/docs/images/smooth.png)

Every Theme is drawn in one of two **Looks**: **Smooth** (the default) or
**Classic**, the flat look of earlier versions. Colours, styling and the
Look are all yours to change; see [Themes](themes.md).

![The same Meters in the Classic Look](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/daswerk/Das-Meter/main/docs/images/classic.png)

The Loudness Meter measures to ITU-R BS.1770-5 and EBU Tech 3341/3342, checked
against the EBU test set.
